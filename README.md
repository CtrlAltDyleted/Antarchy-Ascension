# Antarchy - Ascension

Antarchy - Ascension is a progression-focused Minecraft modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1 built around technology, magic, exploration, automation, boss progression, and large-scale endgame systems.

The pack is centered around Antarchy and combines major progression systems such as Create, Applied Energistics 2, Mekanism, Powah, Industrial Foregoing, PneumaticCraft, Ars Nouveau, Mystical Agriculture, Replication, Extended Crafting, and more.

## Status

Antarchy - Ascension is currently in **Alpha**.

Balance, progression, quests, recipes, configuration, mod selection, and compatibility are still being developed and tested.

Alpha builds are early test releases where the pack is far enough along to benefit from user testing, but major parts of the experience may still change. Alpha builds are not considered finished releases.

During Alpha, users should expect:

- Mods may be added, removed, updated, or replaced
- Recipes, progression, quests, configs, balance, and world generation may change significantly
- Features may be incomplete, experimental, or removed entirely
- Full progression may not be available yet
- Bugs and compatibility issues are expected
- Support for individual Alpha features is not guaranteed
- Worlds created on an Alpha build are not guaranteed to remain compatible with future updates
- Major changes may be made at any time based on testing and feedback

The primary purpose of Alpha releases is to collect high-level feedback and identify major changes that should be made before the pack reaches Beta.

### Beta Expectations

Beta releases are broader test builds where the pack is expected to be mostly complete. Testing during Beta focuses more heavily on bugs, tuning, balancing, progression issues, and real-world playtesting.

Beta users can generally expect the final release to resemble the Beta experience and can typically expect full progression to be available. However:

- Bugs may still exist
- Balance and progression may still change
- Features may still be changed or removed
- World compatibility between Beta updates is still not guaranteed

## Progression

The intended progression moves through several major stages:

- Vanilla exploration and early survival
- Create and mechanical automation
- Early electrical power
- Powah and general FE infrastructure
- Storage and logistics
- Applied Energistics 2
- Industrial Foregoing, PneumaticCraft, and Ars Nouveau
- Mekanism and advanced processing
- Nuclear and fusion power
- Mystical Agriculture and advanced resource automation
- Advanced AE2 systems
- Replication
- Extended Crafting
- Creative and endgame content

Antarchy's materials, bosses, exploration, and dimensions are intended to be integrated throughout the progression rather than existing as a separate content branch.

## Exploration

The pack includes extensive exploration and world-generation content across the Overworld and multiple dimensions.

Major exploration paths include:

- The Aether
- Antarchy dimensions
- Ad Astra
- Infinite Dimensions
- The Nether
- The End

Structures, bosses, dungeons, and dimensional progression are intended to remain relevant alongside the technological progression of the pack.

## Quests

FTB Quests is used to guide progression, introduce major systems, and connect the different technology, magic, exploration, and automation branches of the pack.

The quest system is still under development.

## Repository Contents

This repository contains the files used to develop and configure Antarchy - Ascension, including:

- `config/` - shared mod configuration
- `defaultconfigs/` - default world/server configuration
- `kubejs/` - KubeJS configuration and future custom scripts
- `.gitattributes` - repository line-ending rules
- `.gitignore` - rules preventing local Minecraft and CurseForge data from entering the repository

Additional pack-specific scripts, quests, resources, and customization may be added as development continues.
