# Antarchy - Ascension

Antarchy - Ascension is a progression-focused Minecraft modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1 built around technology, magic, exploration, automation, boss progression, and large-scale endgame systems.

The pack combines major progression systems including Create, Applied Energistics 2, Mekanism, Powah, Industrial Foregoing, PneumaticCraft, Ars Nouveau, Mystical Agriculture, Replication, Extended Crafting, and many others into a connected progression path centered around Antarchy's own content.

> **Alpha Notice:** Antarchy - Ascension is currently in Alpha. Major changes are expected, progression is still under development, and worlds created during Alpha are not guaranteed to remain compatible with future versions.

## Status

Antarchy - Ascension is currently targeting **0.2.0-alpha**.

Alpha builds are functional test releases intended for broader playtesting while development continues. Core systems are playable, but progression, quests, recipes, configuration, balancing, compatibility, and mod selection may still change substantially.

During Alpha, users should expect:

- Mods may be added, removed, updated, or replaced
- Recipes and progression may change significantly
- Quests may be incomplete or reorganized (or not even started!)
- Configuration and balance may change
- World generation may change
- Features may be experimental, incomplete, or removed
- Bugs and compatibility issues may occur
- Full intended progression may not yet be available
- World compatibility between Alpha versions is not guaranteed

The primary purpose of Alpha releases is to identify major gameplay, progression, stability, compatibility, and usability issues before the pack reaches Beta.

### Beta Expectations

Beta releases will represent a more complete version of the intended pack.

Testing during Beta will focus more heavily on:

- Bugs
- Balance
- Progression tuning
- Quest quality
- Multiplayer behavior
- Performance
- Long-term playtesting

Beta builds are expected to provide substantially more complete progression, but features, recipes, balance, and world generation may still change before the final release.

World compatibility between Beta versions is still not guaranteed.

## Progression

Antarchy - Ascension is designed around a connected progression system rather than a collection of independent mods.

The intended progression moves through several major stages:

- Vanilla exploration and early survival
- Create and mechanical automation
- Early electrical power
- Powah and general FE infrastructure
- Storage and logistics
- Applied Energistics 2
- Industrial Foregoing
- PneumaticCraft
- Ars Nouveau
- Mekanism and advanced ore processing
- Nuclear and fusion power
- Mystical Agriculture and advanced resource automation
- Advanced Applied Energistics 2 systems
- Replication
- Extended Crafting
- Endgame progression
- Creative-tier content

Antarchy's materials, bosses, dimensions, structures, and exploration systems are intended to remain relevant throughout technological progression rather than existing as a separate content branch.

## Exploration

The pack includes extensive exploration, structures, bosses, dungeons, and world-generation content across the Overworld and multiple dimensions.

Major exploration paths include:

- The Overworld
- The Nether
- The End
- The Aether
- Antarchy dimensions
- Ad Astra
- Infinite Dimensions

Exploration and dimensional progression are intended to remain useful throughout the pack rather than becoming obsolete once automation develops.

## Technology, Automation, and Magic

Major systems include:

- Create
- Applied Energistics 2
- Mekanism
- Powah
- Industrial Foregoing
- PneumaticCraft
- Mystical Agriculture
- Replication
- Extended Crafting

Major magical content includes systems such as Ars Nouveau along with additional magical equipment, resources, exploration, and progression from other included mods.

These systems will interact through progression, resource requirements, infrastructure, processing chains, storage, and endgame crafting.

## Quests

FTB Quests is used to guide progression, introduce major systems, explain important mechanics, and connect the pack's technology, magic, exploration, automation, and boss progression.

The quest system is still under active development during Alpha.

## Antarchy - Ascension

The pack includes the custom **Antarchy - Ascension** mod.

The custom mod provides pack-specific content, integration, compatibility fixes, and behavior that cannot be handled cleanly through normal configuration or datapacks alone.

It is developed specifically for Antarchy - Ascension and will evolve alongside the pack.
