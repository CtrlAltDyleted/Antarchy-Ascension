# End Remastered portal-frame assets

This Antarchy - Ascension resource pack includes the following unmodified textures
from End Remastered Forge 5.3.3 (Minecraft 1.20.x), by Team Remastered:

- `assets/endrem/textures/block/ancient_portal_frame_empty_top.png`
- `assets/endrem/textures/block/ancient_portal_frame_empty_side.png`

Antarchy - Ascension changed the End Remastered 6.3.0 frame resources on
2026-09-21. The two PNG files are unmodified. The English name override is an Antarchy - Ascension addition.

## Animated portal eyes

The 16 `assets/endrem/textures/block/eyes/*_eye.png` animations and 14 item
animations use artwork from **End Remastered Remastered 1.21.1**, credited in
its pack metadata to J677. Blink timing and eyelid motion come from
**EnderEyes 26.2 v3.1**, credited in its pack metadata to Futureazoo.
Antarchy maps each item blink onto the two 8-by-8 eye faces in the current
End Remastered model. The animated eyelids use colors from the Remastered eye
edges; the remaining texture faces stay identical to the Remastered originals.
The Cryptic, Evil, Guardian, and Witch Eyes close from top and bottom. The
other block eyes close from the sides, following their item animations.
The Magical and Wither Eye item animations are copied unchanged from the
Remastered pack, including their animation metadata. The source packs remain
unchanged in `resourcepacks` and `config/openloader/packs`. The block-eye
textures are 32 pixels wide to match End Remastered 6.3.0's portal-eye model.
